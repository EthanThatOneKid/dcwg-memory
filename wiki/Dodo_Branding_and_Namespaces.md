# Dodo branding, namespaces, and website

This page collects the identity work recorded in `#general` and its threads: package scopes, the command name, domains, the logo, and the website. It is mostly logistics, and nothing here is final. Times are UTC.

## Package scopes and the command

- **JSR scopes, 2026-10-15.** `andy` said he would take the `are-we-dodo-yet` JSR scope, and `CaptainLach` asked whether it is too long to publish under. `Steel` suggested also claiming `@dodo` and `@std-dodo` before someone else does. `CaptainLach` said `@dodo` was already captured by him. `andy` could not take `@std-dodo` because he had hit his scopes quota, which had already been raised to 5 for him; `Steel` grabbed it "just in case." This echoes the namespace table in [Ende versus Dodo](Ende_vs_Dodo.md), which found `dodo` free on JSR and taken elsewhere.
- **The runner command.** `andy` floated calling the equivalent of `dx` or `npx` `dodoer`, noting `ddx` as the alternative, and said the point was that it is funny. The `#dodoer` channel exists because of the same joke. `andy` said in that channel that "dodoer" is not a team name, just a funny word.

## Domain

- **Situation.** On 2026-10-17 `andy` said the website was still waiting on a domain and art. `netop://ウィビ` suggested `dodo.works` and `dodo.live`, both taken, then `dodo.js.org`, which `andy` disliked as dated and bloated-sounding. `andy` proposed `dodo.rocks`, which `Max P.` said was taken. `netop://ウィビ` said that of an affordable list from a registrar's availability filter, a $25 option fit best, a figure that is his reading of the registrar's page. `Steel` noted on 2026-10-15 that the `dodo.space` top-level domain is expensive and most others are taken, and that `do.do` is unavailable. `Max P.` asked why so many dodo domains are taken.
- **Registered already.** `Stag Math` pointed to a message in `#general` saying the group has a handful of domains registered. The message is not reproduced in the capture.
- **Visibility.** `CaptainLach` said domain selection should not be discussed in public channels, and `netop://ウィビ` replied "Delete this! I just edited," apparently about his own message listing options. This page lists no purchase decisions for that reason.
- **Funding.** `daniellacosse` said on 2026-10-15 that he was thinking of applying to OSC and maybe splitting it, since the project would need a legal entity for things like an Apple license. OSC is not expanded in the capture.

## Logo

`visualbam` proposed a logo on 2026-10-12 as an evolution of Deno's existing mark. The discussion:

- Lowercase versus capital "d." `visualbam` said he tried keeping Deno's font, and that the lowercase form looked better in it. `daniellacosse` found the lowercase version "adult" and suggested trying a capital, and later suggested keeping the lowercase but making every character the same height, as Deno's font does. `visualbam` tried variations and found them awkward.
- `Ooker` suggested italics. `daniellacosse` considered a bird's head on the "d" and decided it was too much.
- `visualbam` offered an asset pack with a Discord icon as a temporary logo, joking that Deno core team members who join would think "wait a minute." `daniellacosse` agreed it was great as a stopgap, but that a lasting logo would need a more custom font.
- `Stag Math`, who had offered to help with logo work, said it looked good as is and was away on a work trip.
- On 2026-10-23 `SPOOKY day` asked why the logo's background is not perfectly black. `daniellacosse` answered that tinting blacks and whites to fit a color scheme is common, and that Deno's is not pure black either, then fixed it. `CaptainLach` noted the Discord logo is just transparent. No one took the stand that the logo is final: `daniellacosse` said that if the project has to break off in its own identity, the logo can be revisited.

## Website

- **2026-10-14.** `andy` said he was working on a website for Dodo, "just a shitty thing to throw up for SEO" for now. `daniellacosse` nudged `Stag Math` about the assets it would need, and `andy` said he would try to make it look nice. On 2026-10-15 he shared the repository, `are-we-dodo-yet/dodo_web`.[^1]
- **Illustrations.** `andy` asked `Stag Math` for two illustrations: an asteroid coming from an orange cloud and hitting the dinosaurs, and a group of dodo birds huddled around a campfire, at a 3:2 aspect ratio and working on light and dark backgrounds. `Stag Math` agreed. On 2026-10-22 `andy` asked about the status, and no answer is in the capture.
- **Messaging.** `netop://ウィビ` argued on 2026-10-17 that Dodo should put a line like Deno's "Deno is the glue" in every blog post, and build its marketing and documentation sites in Dodo, and ask everyone in the server to move their Deno-backed sites to Dodo. `daniellacosse` agreed that demonstrating beats telling, which is the case for the [showcase framework](Killer_App_Framework.md).
- **Press mentions.** `daniellacosse` said on 2026-10-17 that the group should publish more of its plans, because a news article that mentioned Dodo was working from outdated information. `andy` said a v1 could ship without art, and `daniellacosse` started a README cleanup branch. See [Community and press reaction](Community_and_Press_Reaction.md).
