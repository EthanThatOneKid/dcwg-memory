# Community and press reaction

`#general` participants shared the outside coverage they ran into. This page lists what was shared and what the group said about it, with the page titles and descriptions as the embeds in the capture show them. The linked pages were not read for this record, and statements about their content are the participants' or the embeds'. Times are UTC.

## What was shared

- **A Fireship video, 2026-10-15.** `Steel` shared a video whose embed title is "The runtime wars are over... and Node won." Its description says Cloudflare acquired Deno and that the creator of Node.js is admitting reimplementing it was a mistake. `daniellacosse` invited anyone to read the comments about Dodo, and `ZombieB` shared it again on 2026-10-18. `netop://ウィビ` said the author's memes are always top-tier, and `loading...` and `paula` speculated about whether he writes his own videos.
- **A Socket blog post, 2026-10-17.** `Max P.` shared a post titled "Deno Goes the Way of the Dinosaur" and suggested searching it for "dodo." `daniellacosse` said the post was working from already-outdated information and read as AI-generated, while it was cool to see Dodo mentioned, and that the group should make more of its plans public. `netop://ウィビ` was amused that it used a quote from him.
- **Deno's own response.** `netop://ウィビ` quoted a line from Deno's blog post "Reports of Deno's Demise Have Been Greatly Exaggerated," that Deno gives you a permissions system, a web server, observability, linting, and type safety out of the box, and said every post should say something like it. `daniellacosse` said the group should demonstrate this rather than just say it.
- **A video from a streamer, 2026-10-19.** `SpaceMonkey` shared a video about Deno's situation, which `taco` said was what led him to find the server.
- **Cloudflare and Vite, 2026-10-20.** After `SpaceMonkey` reported that an AI assistant had suggested the group try to acquire the Deno name from Cloudflare, with the Node trademark precedent as a point of comparison, and said he had not known Cloudflare had bought Vite and Astro, `daniellacosse` corrected the second point. He linked a Cloudflare post titled "VoidZero is joining Cloudflare," quoting that Cloudflare is committing $1 million to a Vite ecosystem fund administered by the Vite core team, and said Cloudflare acquihired the teams and did not buy the software. Nothing in the capture says whether the group will approach Cloudflare about the Deno name.
- **Reddit, 2026-10-22 and 2026-10-23.** `marc` shared a Reddit post about Bart's comment on a foundation. `daniellacosse` shared a comment on it from someone who said he migrated every project to Node.js and no longer sees a point in Deno, and said comments like that are why the project must push Deno forward and not just maintain it: "we need something like embeddability to bring people back." See [Foundation outreach and Bart's reply](Foundation_Outreach.md).
- **A RedMonk post, 2026-10-27.** `netop://ウィビ` said it mentions Dodo and Ende. Its embed title is "The Web Can't Afford a JavaScript Runtime Monoculture," and its description says Deno announced its whole team is joining Cloudflare and that development of the runtime stops after one year, leaving two major general-purpose runtimes.
- **Mozilla, 2026-10-21.** `Leokuma` asked whether Mozilla might take over Deno development. `Seally` and `Sandeep Kumar Kuanar✌🏼` said Mozilla is in enough financial trouble and no longer owns Rust, so they did not expect it. Those are opinions.
- **JSR's future, 2026-10-27.** `paula` asked what happens to JSR, since its frontend is built on Deno's Fresh framework, and quoted the announcement's line that "JSR will continue operating, with its infrastructure moving to Cloudflare." `netop://ウィビ` said they had said little beyond Bart mentioning foundations. See [JSR and the package registry](JSR_and_Package_Registry.md).

## Which forks exist, as the group describes them

On 2026-10-20 `Doctor 🤖` asked whether three forks of Deno were being discussed. `CaptainLach` said only two, Dodo and Ende, and that `#andromeda` is "some other thing, unrelated to Deno," which `daniellacosse` clarified is not a fork but a pure Rust runtime whose developers compare notes with the group. `CaptainLach` described Dodo as a Deno continuation focused on extensibility and embeddability that does not break compatibility. On 2026-10-26 `andy` told `paula` that Dodo is the primary fork and Ende intentionally strips Node support, adding that Ende may be his own primary runtime. `daniellacosse` told her it was safe to switch to Ende for now if she used no Node APIs. See [Ende versus Dodo](Ende_vs_Dodo.md).
