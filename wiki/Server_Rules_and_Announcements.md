# Server rules and announcements

Two small channels hold the working group's rules and its announcements. Both were first archived on 2026-10-29, so everything here is read from the capture, with UTC times.

## The rules

`andy` posted four rules to `#rules` on 2026-10-11 and edited the post on 2026-10-15:

1. don't be a jerk
2. be constructive
3. if you're here exclusively for self-promotion, go somewhere else
4. try to avoid fragmentation

The fourth rule is the one that bears on the project. It is the same instinct as `daniellacosse`'s suggestion elsewhere that [Ende](Ende_Runtime.md) and Dodo might collaborate on a shared standard library rather than each building one. See [Ende versus Dodo](Ende_vs_Dodo.md).

## The announcements

`#news` holds eight messages from three participants, between 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-18.

- **2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13.** Two posts, one from `andy` and one from `daniellacosse`, have no text content in the capture; Discord system notices of unknown kind surround them. The record does not say what they announced.
- **The first meeting, 2026-10-13.** An announcement with a Discord event link read "our first meeting! See y'all there!" On 2026-10-15 at 18:02Z another read "@everyone meeting is starting now!" Both appear under `daniellacosse`'s block in the capture, so he is their author. The meeting's content is not recorded here, but a participant who missed it asked for notes on 2026-10-15 and `daniellacosse` posted his notes in `#brainstorm`, which is archived.
- **Onboarding, 2026-10-15.** `andy` announced that the server had enabled onboarding, that future announcements would go out only to people with a particular role, and that members should give themselves that role if they want to be pinged.
- **dodo-bindgen, 2026-10-18.** `CaptainLach` announced that `deno_bindgen`, an official but long-dormant way of writing FFI bindings, had been revived under Dodo with a better implementation inspired by `uniffi-rs` and `wasm-bindgen` and better support for Dodo's and Deno's own `dlopen`, and linked `are-we-dodo-yet/dodo-bindgen`. He pitched it for binding to system and Rust ecosystem libraries for things like USB and audio. See [Rust core rewrite proposal](Rust_Core_Proposal.md).
