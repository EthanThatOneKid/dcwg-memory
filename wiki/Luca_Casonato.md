# Luca Casonato

Named in the group's channel on 2026-10-11 as the person worth asking for advice, because he is the one who has already moved a Deno flagship project out of `denoland`.

## Why the group raised his name

`W7T2A` proposed reaching out to him, on the grounds that he left Deno but was one of the project's main contributors. Max P. agreed and added the specific reason: he moved Fresh into an independent organization, so his experience of a handoff is worth something even if he cannot contribute code.

## Verified background

- Former Deno core team member; GitHub profile now reads "Working @railwayapp. Prev @denoland core team. @tc39 delegate", with 2,386 followers as of 2026-10-11.
- Creator of Fresh, the framework that became the group's handoff precedent. See [[Fresh_Framework_Migration]].
- TC39 delegate for Deno, and a participant in WinterCG, the W3C community group that Cloudflare, Deno, Shopify and others started to standardize server-side JavaScript runtime APIs.
- Spoke about Deno and Deno Deploy on Software Engineering Radio episode 553 (2023), where the runtime-portability argument that the group is now revisiting is laid out.

## What he could answer

- How the Fresh repository move was negotiated, and what Deno Land Inc. kept.
- Whether the JSR publisher scope was deliberately left on `denoland` or simply not updated, which determines how much work a runtime handoff has to do at the registry layer.
- Whether a foundation umbrella was considered for Fresh, and if not, why not.

## Open questions

- The group has not reported contacting him. The two members who raised his name are the only ones on record.
- His current employer is Railway, not Deno, so any contribution would be advice rather than custody of Deno assets.
