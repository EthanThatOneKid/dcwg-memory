# Projects that depend on Deno

On 2026-10-11 (UTC) `daniellacosse` wondered in `#general` whether it would be a good idea to maintain a list of people who depend on Deno, who could be potential allies. `w` replied the same evening with a list he compiled from GitHub's dependents view for `denoland/deno`, filtered to repositories with more than 100 stars or more than 50 forks.

## The caveats `w` gave

He said he was uncertain whether the exercise is fruitful, that it "certainly isn't supabase-type disclosure," meaning it is not a public statement of reliance by a company, but that it might be good reconnaissance. The page does not know what the dependents view counts. It lists repositories that depend on the `denoland/deno` repository as GitHub models it, and whether each uses the runtime was not checked.

## What was said about using it

`daniellacosse` replied that he used to be a core contributor to an open-source project that successfully transitioned to a foundation, and that he would try to get in touch with the people who did that, adding that lists like this are very helpful if the group goes that route. He later cited a contact who had converted key open-source projects into foundations in the email to Deno; see [Foundation outreach and Bart's reply](Foundation_Outreach.md).
