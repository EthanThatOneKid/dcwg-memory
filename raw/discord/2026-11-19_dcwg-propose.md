## Next steps to formalize community (thread 1572787160032084020)

**Ethan:Print"Ethan";:GoTo Ethan** — 2026-11-19T01:34:39Z (`1572787160032084020`)

Hey everyone 🙂

Things have been moving a little slowly. Part of that is we don't have proper channels to make group decisions. Who decides what LTS means, who runs the test suite and releases, who holds the repos/trademark, who talks to the Deno team? And who controls the money? Any funding stuff is basically blocked until we figure out the structure.

My starting proposals:
- W3C Community Group: Public home for the LTS baseline + test suite. Wouldn't own a runtime, hold a trademark, or handle money, but it's a good start. ([AI-assisted proposal drafts](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_umRmK5mO5Sci7Tg9SBO5rCsDadO_O0x))
- Start our own nonprofit foundation: Can hold a trademark and money, but it's a complicated legal process.
- Informal nonprofit board: elect a president, VP, treasurer, secretary, have meetings. Structure in place without the legal nonsense.
- Just continue to use the GitHub org issue board <https://github.com/orgs/are-we-dodo-yet/projects/2>: mods of Dodo keep it up to date, which is where we are now, an interest group keeping things warm while Deno's foundation plays out. Mods post regular polls and uphold what people vote for.

We already have the `are-we-dodo-yet` org on GitHub and it's where the code lives either way. 

If you've got insight into better ideas, or you don't like these, drop it in a thread. Just keep it concise: what is it, what problem does it solve, who runs it, and who controls the money if there is any.

If you agree with a more formal structure in general, hit ✅ on this post. (no vote for any one option until then.)

I'll leave this open till Fri Nov 20, 5pm PT, then we'll do a proper poll to narrow it down. <@400711308141658112>, want to run that poll?

Thanks all.
