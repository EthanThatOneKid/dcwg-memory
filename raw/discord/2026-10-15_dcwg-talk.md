This is not an attack on someone but I do want to share my view on environmental opinion around LLMs.

Recently I had a call with a recruiter specialising in sustainable and eco startups, and I was looking for a coding job in that industry.

I did ask, is there a conflict in interest with an eco startup using LLMs for code gen. And the recruiters answer was that, considering the good that the organisations do, it was a net positive for them to use LLMs. I followed up with, do they measure their use. And her answer was no - its obvious that they are providing a net good.

It took a few months for that to marinade and for me to realise I wasn't satisfied with the answer. How can you justify doing something that is "a bit bad", by saying the end result is "a lot good".

Only speaking for myself, I want to measure, and know the damage I am doing, and also measure the end result, be it social benefit or improved efficiencies for users. Then I'll work out a methodology to compare the apples to oragnges.

I am by no means shouting from an ivory tower, hell I drive a gas guzzler car. But I think its fair to listen to all concerns. Speaking from experience , I have had crippling anxiety around environmental issues, so I can relate to <@198537833345056768> . I can also relate to devs using LLMs because do, and have done for the past year or so without a second thought.

I vote for progress, but not at all costs.
